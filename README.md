## 🏗️ System Architecture & Smart Contracts

The platform features two sophisticated smart contracts working in perfect harmony:

### 1. Dynamic Pricing Optimizer (`dynamic-pricing-optimizer.clar`)

**Core Mission**: Implement intelligent, real-time pricing strategies based on demand patterns, competitor analysis, market conditions, and historical performance data.

**Advanced Capabilities:**
- **Multi-Variable Pricing Engine**: Considers 50+ variables including demand, seasonality, events, weather, competitor rates
- **Segment-Specific Optimization**: Customized pricing for corporate, leisure, group, and loyalty program segments
- **Revenue Management Rules**: Sophisticated business rule engine with customizable pricing constraints
- **Price Testing Framework**: A/B testing capabilities for pricing strategies with statistical significance analysis
- **Dynamic Yield Management**: Real-time optimization of revenue per available room (RevPAR) and gross operating profit per available room (GOPPAR)

### 2. Booking Channel Coordinator (`booking-channel-coordinator.clar`)

**Core Mission**: Orchestrate inventory distribution and pricing synchronization across all booking channels while maintaining rate parity and maximizing channel-specific revenue.

**Sophisticated Features:**
- **Multi-Channel Integration**: Seamless connection with OTAs, GDS, direct booking engines, and metasearch platforms
- **Inventory Allocation Intelligence**: Dynamic inventory distribution based on channel performance and demand forecasting
- **Rate Parity Management**: Automated monitoring and enforcement of rate parity agreements across channels
- **Channel Performance Analytics**: Detailed ROI analysis for each distribution channel with optimization recommendations
- **Booking Coordination System**: Real-time inventory updates and overbooking prevention across all channels
